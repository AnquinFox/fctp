# Hainan 30-Day Visa-Free: How to Enter, Stay & Extend (2026 Guide)

![Wuzhizhou Island, Hainan — clear water and white sand](/images/foreigners/china_hainan_wuzhizhou_island_beach_clear_water_056.webp)

Imagine landing in China with **no visa, no paperwork, and 30 days of tropical island** in front of you. That's Hainan.

Sixty-one countries can walk into China's only tropical island province — beaches, palm trees, rainforest, duty-free malls — and stay a full month. It is, without exaggeration, the easiest way into China. ✨

But there are two rules that trip people up: **you can't leave Hainan**, and **the 30 days run fast**. Let me walk you through the whole thing. 🌴

---

## 🌍 Who Qualifies? (61 Countries)

Citizens of **61 countries** can enter Hainan visa-free for **up to 30 days**:

| Region | Countries |
|--------|-----------|
| **Europe** | Russia, UK, France, Germany, Norway, Ukraine, Italy, Austria, Finland, Netherlands, Denmark, Switzerland, Sweden, Spain, Belgium, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Ireland, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Romania, Serbia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, Monaco, Belarus |
| **Americas** | USA, Canada, Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Chile |
| **Oceania** | Australia, New Zealand |
| **Asia** | South Korea, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Kazakhstan, UAE, Brunei, Qatar, Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Kyrgyzstan |

> ⚠️ **US citizens:** this is one of your easiest routes into China without a visa. You qualify for the **Hainan scheme**, even though you're not on the national visa-free list.

**What you can do:** tourism, business, visiting, seeing family, **medical treatment**, exhibitions, sports.
**What you can't:** work or study.

## ✈️ Getting In: Ports & Paperwork

![Tianya Haijiao rocks on the Hainan coast at sunset](/images/foreigners/china_hainan_tianya_haijiao_rocks_sunset_058.webp)

### The ports

You can enter through **any of Hainan's open ports**:

- ✈️ **Haikou Meilan** International Airport
- ✈️ **Sanya Phoenix** International Airport
- ✈️ **Qionghai Boao** Airport
- 🚢 Haikou Port, Sanya Port, Yangpu Port, Qinglan Port, Basuo Port

### Do you need to pre-register?

**No.** There's no advance declaration — you just show up with your passport and pass through border inspection. ✅

> 💡 **No direct flight to Hainan?** Fly to a country (or Hong Kong / Macau) that *does* have a direct route, then transit straight into Hainan. This is common for European travellers.

### What to bring

| Item | Needed? |
|------|---------|
| Passport | ✅ Must be valid |
| Visa | ❌ Not for eligible nationalities |
| Return ticket | ⚠️ Recommended — officers may ask how you'll leave |
| Hotel booking | ⚠️ Recommended, and you must register your address anyway |
| Onward ticket to a third country | ❌ Not required (that's the *transit* scheme, not this one) |

> 📌 **Key difference from the 240-hour transit scheme:** Hainan doesn't require a third-country ticket. You can fly in and fly straight home.

## 📅 Day 1 · Landing & First Impressions

### 🎡 玩 (Play)

Land, clear border inspection, and get to your hotel. If you're in Sanya, head straight to **Yalong Bay** for a first swim — the water is calm and the sand is fine.

![Yalong Bay beach in Sanya with blue sea and sand](/images/foreigners/sanya_yalong_bay_beach_blue_sea_023.webp)

### 🚕 行 (Get around)

Metro is limited outside Haikou; **DiDi** (in Alipay) and the **Hainan Circular High-Speed Rail** are your friends. The ring railway circles the whole island — Haikou to Sanya in about 1.5–2 hours.

### 🥥 吃 (Eat)

Hainan chicken rice (文昌鸡), coconut chicken hotpot, and fresh seafood at the night markets. The island's food is light, coconut-forward and genuinely excellent.

## 🌊 Day 2–3 · Sanya & the South

### 🎡 玩 (Play)

- **Wuzhizhou Island** — the clearest water in Hainan; snorkelling and diving
- **Nanshan** — the towering 108m Guanyin statue over the sea
- **Yalong Bay** — resort zone, calm water, best for families

![The Nanshan Guanyin statue over the sea in Hainan](/images/foreigners/china_hainan_nanshan_guanyin_buddha_sea_057.webp)

### 🚕 行

Sanya is spread out — DiDi everywhere, or hire a driver for the day (¥400–600) to string the sights together.

### 🥥 吃

Seafood markets where you pick your fish and they cook it — the classic Sanya experience.

## ⛵ Day 4–5 · Coast, Marina & Slow Down

### 🎡 玩 (Play)

- **Banshan Peninsula** — quiet marina, yachts, and the best sunsets on the island
- A **duty-free mall** run (Hainan's duty-free allowance is generous)
- Or just do nothing on the beach, which is also the correct answer

### 🚕 行

Banshan is a short drive from Sanya city. Hotels run shuttles; DiDi works too.

### 🥥 吃

Beachfront BBQ and coconut water. You're on holiday.

## ⚠️ The Two Rules That Matter

![Dadonghai Bay beach in Sanya, sunny coast with palms](/images/foreigners/sanya_dadonghai_beach_bay_sunny_coast_palm_008.webp)

### 1. You cannot leave Hainan

The visa-free entry covers **Hainan province only**. Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou — off limits without separate permission.

**If you want to travel onward into mainland China**, you must apply in advance at a Hainan public security exit-entry office for the proper visa/stay permit. Do this **before your 30 days expire**, with supporting documents.

### 2. The 30 days start the day *after* you arrive

The clock runs from **00:00 the day after entry**. So an arrival on the 1st gives you until the end of the 30th. Plan your exit flight accordingly — **don't overstay**.

### Extending your stay

If you have a legitimate reason to stay longer (or to leave Hainan for the mainland), apply at the Hainan exit-entry authority **at least 7 days before your stay expires**. Bring your passport, accommodation registration, and supporting documents.

## 🧳 Practical Bits

- **Payments:** Alipay and WeChat Pay work everywhere. Set them up before you fly — see our [payment guide](/guides/foreigners/payment-setup/).
- **Your phone:** an eSIM is the easiest internet route — see the [internet & VPN guide](/guides/foreigners/internet-vpn/).
- **Register your address** within 24 hours; hotels do it automatically.
- **Weather:** Nov–Apr is the sweet spot. Summer is hot and humid with typhoon risk.
- **Duty-free:** Hainan has one of the world's most generous duty-free allowances — bring your passport to the mall.

> 🔗 **Staying only a few days and flying onward to a third country?** The [240-hour visa-free transit](/guides/foreigners/china-240-hour-visa-free-transit-guide/) might suit you better. Compare with the [full visa & entry guide](/guides/foreigners/visa-entry/).

## 🎯 The Bottom Line

Hainan is the **easiest visa-free entry into China** — 61 countries, 30 days, no pre-registration, no third-country ticket needed. Just remember: you're on an island, legally speaking. Stay in Hainan, watch the clock, and extend in advance if you need more.

Then go enjoy the palm trees. 🌴☀️
