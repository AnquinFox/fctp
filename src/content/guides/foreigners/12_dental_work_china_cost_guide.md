## 📝 How To Book (Do This Before You Fly)

![Modern Shanghai skyline with dental tourism clinics downtown](/images/foreigners/shanghai_lujiazui_skyline_modern_district_014.webp)

**1️⃣ Find an international dental clinic.** Search "Shanghai international dental clinic English" — look for one with English service pages and foreign-patient reviews. JCI-accredited hospitals with dental departments are the safest bet.

**2️⃣ Email them.** Send name, nationality, what you want done, and any dental X-rays you have. Keep it short.

**3️⃣ Get a written treatment plan + quote.** Ask for: number of visits, days between visits, and total in ¥ with a USD reference. **Get this in writing.** This is the single most important step 💯

**4️⃣ Book your window.** Most work needs **two sittings**, 3–7 days apart (they scan on day one, fit on the last day).

**5️⃣ Sort visa + payment.** A tourist visa is fine. Set up Alipay/WeChat Pay before you land.

> ⚠️ If a clinic is dramatically cheaper than everyone else, ask WHY. Rock-bottom usually means a lab tech you'd rather not meet.

## 🗓️ The 6-Day Itinerary (Dental + Shanghai)

![Doctor consulting a patient over a tablet](/images/foreigners/china_doctor_consultation_results_tablet_patient_012.webp)

**Day 1 — Land & settle** ✈️
Fly into Pudong or Hongqiao. Check into a hotel or service apartment near the clinic. Do a light dinner, sleep off the jet lag.

**Day 2 — Consultation & scan** 🦷
Morning appointment: 3D scan, exam, confirm the plan and final quote. Usually done in 1–2 hours. Afternoon: walk the Bund.

![The Bund waterfront skyline at evening](/images/foreigners/shanghai_bund_waterfront_skyline_evening_travel_001.webp)

**Day 3 — Free day (the fun one)** 🚶
No clinic today. Old town, teahouses, riverside sunset. Eat everything you love that's *soft* — save the crunchy stuff for after.

**Day 4 — Main procedure** 🪥
The big sitting: implants placed, or prep + temporary fit. You'll want an easy afternoon. Soup, congee, an early night.

**Day 5 — Fit & polish** ✨
Final fitting, adjustments, bite check. If it's crowns/veneers, here's where you walk out new.

**Day 6 — Buffer + fly home** 🛫
Leave a buffer day. If anything feels off, you want time to go back in — not to be at an airport gate.

> 💡 Book the buffer. Always. Airlines don't care about your bite.

## 🍲 What To Eat While Your Mouth Heals

![Clean modern clinic reception lobby](/images/foreigners/china_hospital_lobby_reception_desk_clean_011.webp)

This is the part people don't plan for 🥲 Save this:

- 🍚 **Congee** — the classic. Cheap, everywhere, perfect.
- 🥚 **Steamed egg custard** — silky, protein, zero chewing.
- 🍜 **Soft noodles** — skip the chilli oil on day one.
- 🥣 **Warm soups** — nothing too hot.
- 🍌 **Bananas + yogurt** — hotel-breakfast heroes.

❌ Skip on healing days: hotpot, crunchy fried stuff, ice-cold drinks, super-spicy, and anything you'd have to *bite into*.

After day 4, treat yourself to a proper Shanghai dinner — you earned it 🎉

## ❓ Quick FAQ

**How long do I need in Shanghai?** 5–7 days for implant/crown work. Whitening or a cleaning = 2–3 days.

**Do they speak English?** At international clinics, yes — front desk to dentist.

**Can I pay by card?** Big clinics take international cards, Alipay, WeChat Pay. Ask first.

**What if something hurts after I fly home?** Get your full records + X-rays in English before you leave, and ask for a contact method. Most clinics do remote follow-up.

**Is this legit?** Very. Medical travel is officially promoted in China, and Shanghai is the country's dental-tourism hub.
