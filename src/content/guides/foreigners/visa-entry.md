# Visa & Entry Guide for Foreigners Coming to China (2026)

![Immigration hall at a Chinese airport](/images/foreigners/china_visa_airport_immigration_hall_042.webp)

China has quietly become one of the easiest countries in the world to enter without a visa. Between unilateral visa-free entry, 240-hour transit, and the Hainan scheme, most travellers from Europe, Asia-Pacific and the Americas can now walk in with nothing but a passport.

But the rules turn over fast, and the details matter. Here's the current picture — verified against the National Immigration Administration (NIA) in **October 2026**.

> ⚠️ **Policies roll over constantly.** Always re-check [nia.gov.cn](https://www.nia.gov.cn) (or the 12367 hotline) before you book, because numbers and dates do change.

---

## The Three Visa-Free Schemes at a Glance

| Scheme | Who | Stay | Where |
|--------|-----|------|-------|
| **Unilateral visa-free** | 50 countries | Up to 30 days | Mainland China |
| **240-hour transit** | 57 countries | Up to 10 days | 24 provinces, 65 ports |
| **Hainan 30-day** | 61 countries | Up to 30 days | Hainan province only |

Different schemes, different rules. Pick yours below.

---

## 1. Unilateral Visa-Free Entry (50 Countries)

Citizens of **50 countries** can enter mainland China **without a visa** for business, tourism, family visits, exchange visits or transit, for **up to 30 days**. The stay counts from **00:00 the day after arrival**.

> ⚠️ **The United States is NOT on this list.** US citizens need a visa, or must use the 240-hour transit scheme.

**Eligible countries (as of 17 February 2026):**

| Region | Countries |
|--------|-----------|
| **Europe (35)** | Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom |
| **Asia (7)** | Bahrain, Brunei, Japan, Kuwait, Oman, South Korea, Saudi Arabia |
| **Oceania (2)** | Australia, New Zealand |
| **Americas (6)** | Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Peru, Uruguay |

> 🔔 **Expiry dates differ by country.** These are pilot arrangements, and **most currently run to 31 December 2026** — but not all. Check your own country's specific end date before relying on it. The UK and Canada joined on 17 February 2026.

## 2. 240-Hour Visa-Free Transit (57 Countries)

![A passport with an entry stamp at immigration](/images/foreigners/china_visa_free_entry_passport_stamp_044.webp)

Citizens of **57 countries** can transit through China visa-free for **up to 240 hours (10 days)**, entering through any of **65 open ports across 24 provinces**.

**The three conditions:**

1. You hold a **valid passport** (and meet the onward-country entry requirements)
2. You have a **confirmed onward ticket** to a **third country or region**
3. You enter through one of the **65 designated ports**

You may travel, do business, visit and see family within the permitted area — but **work, study and journalism still require a visa**.

**Eligible countries (57):**

| Region | Countries |
|--------|-----------|
| **Europe (40)** | Albania, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine, United Kingdom |
| **Americas (6)** | Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Mexico, United States |
| **Oceania (2)** | Australia, New Zealand |
| **Asia (9)** | Brunei, Indonesia, Japan, Kyrgyzstan, Qatar, Singapore, South Korea, United Arab Emirates, Vietnam |

> 🆕 **Newest additions:** Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam (added 20 August 2026).

**Good to know:**

- **65 ports, 24 provinces** — from Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou to Chengdu, Xi'an and beyond
- Stay area is usually the province plus adjacent regions — check your entry port's permitted area
- **24-hour transit visa-free** is separate and simpler: it applies at **all open ports**, for anyone with an onward ticket, as long as you don't leave the port's restricted area

## 3. Hainan 30-Day Visa-Free (61 Countries)

Citizens of **61 countries** can enter **Hainan province** without a visa for up to **30 days**, for tourism, business, visits, family, **medical treatment**, exhibitions and sports — but not work or study.

- **Where:** Hainan province only (entry and exit through any of Hainan's open ports)
- **Who:** 61 countries, including Russia, the UK, most of Europe, the US, Canada, Brazil, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Kazakhstan, the UAE, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam and Kyrgyzstan

> 💡 Hainan is the one scheme that explicitly covers **medical treatment** — relevant if you're combining a trip with a check-up or treatment. See our [medical treatment visa guide](/guides/foreigners/china-medical-visa-entry-guide/).

## 4. Other Regional Schemes

| Scheme | Who | Stay | Area |
|--------|-----|------|------|
| **Cruise groups** | Foreign tour groups (2+) on a cruise | Up to 15 days | 11 coastal provinces + Beijing |
| **ASEAN groups → Xishuangbanna** | ASEAN tour groups (2+) | Up to 6 days | Xishuangbanna, Yunnan |
| **ASEAN groups → Guilin** | ASEAN tour groups (2+) | Up to 6 days | Guilin, Guangxi |
| **HK/Macau groups → Guangdong** | Groups formed in HK/Macau | Up to 6 days | 9 Greater Bay Area cities + Shantou |
| **HK/Macau groups → Hainan** | Groups formed in HK/Macau | Up to 6 days | Hainan |

All group schemes are **organised through a licensed travel agency** and are **group-in, group-out**.

## 5. 🆕 The Arrival Card Can Now Be Filled In Online

![Filling in the arrival card online on a phone](/images/foreigners/china_arrival_card_online_filling_phone_045.webp)

Since **20 November 2025**, you can complete the **foreigner arrival card online before you fly** — no more scrabbling for a pen on the plane.

**Where:** the NIA website, the "NIA 12367" app, or the WeChat / Alipay mini-program (or scan the QR code at the port).

**Who's exempt (7 cases):**

1. Holders of a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID
2. Holders of a Mainland Travel Permit for HK/Macau residents (non-Chinese)
3. Group visa holders, or those entering under group visa-free arrangements
4. 24-hour direct transit that doesn't leave the port's restricted area
5. Cruise passengers entering and leaving on the same ship
6. Fast-lane (e-channel) arrivals
7. Foreign crew of inbound/outbound transport

Everyone else: fill it in online to skip the queue. There's also a paper form if you'd rather.

> ⚠️ **Beware of fake sites.** The official online arrival card is **free**. Scam sites charge a fee — only use the NIA channels.

## 6. If You Do Need a Visa

![Passport and visa application documents](/images/foreigners/china_visa_passport_documents_application_043.webp)

### Common visa types

| Type | Purpose | Typical stay |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **L** | Tourism | 30–90 days |
| **M** | Business / trade | 30–90 days |
| **F** | Short-term exchange / visits | 30–90 days |
| **G** | Transit | 24 h – 10 days |
| **S1 / S2** | Visiting family in China / private affairs | S1: 90+ days · S2: short |
| **Q1 / Q2** | Family reunion with Chinese relatives | Q1: 90+ days · Q2: short |

### How to apply

![Visa application service centre](/images/foreigners/china_visa_application_center_desk_046.webp)

1. Fill in the form online at [visaforchina.org](https://www.visaforchina.org)
2. Book an appointment at your nearest Chinese embassy / consulate or visa centre
3. Submit documents, give fingerprints (if required), pay the fee
4. Processing: typically 4–7 working days; express 2–3 days

### Document checklist

- [ ] Passport — 6+ months validity, blank pages
- [ ] Completed visa application form
- [ ] Recent photo (48mm × 33mm, white background)
- [ ] Flight itinerary and hotel bookings
- [ ] Proof of funds (3–6 months of bank statements)
- [ ] Travel insurance covering China
- [ ] Purpose-specific documents (invitation letter, etc.)

---

## 7. Arrival Procedures

1. **Immigration** — passport plus visa, or proof of visa-free eligibility (onward ticket, etc.)
2. **Arrival card** — filled in online, or on the paper form
3. **Baggage claim**
4. **Customs** — declare anything over the allowance

**Keep handy:** passport, visa (if any), hotel confirmation, onward ticket, travel insurance.

## 9. Pro Tips

1. **Register your accommodation within 24 hours.** Hotels do it automatically; if you stay with friends, you must register at the local police station.
2. **Carry your passport.** Hotels, train tickets and spot checks all need it.
3. **Keep your onward ticket accessible** if you're using visa-free transit.
4. **Download the NIA app** ("移民局") for visa-free status and immigration queries.
5. **Don't overstay.** Even one day can mean a fine and a mark against future entries.
